The Streamer's Arsenal

Command Center

Your central hub. Monitor viewers, run polls, and trigger quick actions without leaving the window. Includes built-in protection against link spam and caps abuse.

  • ✓ Live Chat Feed
  • ✓ Quick Action Buttons (Raid/Clip/Poll)
  • ✓ Stream Health Stats

Integrated Soundboard

Trigger sounds directly from your keyboard (F13-F24) or via Twitch Channel Points. Includes advanced audio routing and emergency controls.

  • ✓ Panic Button: Instantly stop all audio.
  • ✓ Output Routing: Send audio to GoXLR/Virtual Cables.
  • ✓ Supports .MP3, .WAV, .OGG

Widget Store

Expand your stream with our growing library of interactive widgets. Start with the "Omni-Bar" today, or drop physics-based bubbles on your screen.

  • ✓ Chat Bubbles: Display chat messages on screen.
  • ✓ Death Counter: Track fails with custom visuals.
  • ✓ Omni-Bar: Gamify your sub/bit goals.
